Two things have been living on the staff table for weeks, and they're finally on the menu.

## Mishti Doi Cheesecake (BDT 350)

Nabila makes the mishti doi herself, in clay pots, every other morning. It goes into a baked
cheesecake on a biscuit base, slow and low, so the caramel notes of the doi come through rather
than getting lost under cream cheese. It's not too sweet. It is very hard to share.

## Cold Brew with Date Molasses (BDT 320)

Our cold brew steeps for eighteen hours. We sweeten it with khejur gur, the date-palm molasses
that turns up in winter, and pour it over ice. It tastes like a Dhaka winter morning, served
cold.

Both are available from today, while they last each day. Ask at the counter what's left after 3 PM.
