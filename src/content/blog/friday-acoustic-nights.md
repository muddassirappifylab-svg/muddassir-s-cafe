Every Friday from 8:00 to 10:00 PM, the corner by the plants becomes a small stage. We invite
singers and guitarists from around Dhanmondi to play two short sets: Rabindra Sangeet one week,
old Bangla band songs the next, sometimes something new.

## How it works

- There's no cover charge. Order what you like from the full menu.
- We open on Fridays at 2:00 PM, and the evening tables fill quickly.
  [Book a table](/book/) and mention "acoustic night" so we seat you within earshot.
- Want to play? Send us a short recording on WhatsApp and we'll find you a Friday.

See you on Friday.
