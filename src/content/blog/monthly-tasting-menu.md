Once a month, Rafiq picks one ingredient from the bazaar and builds seven small courses around
it. Last time it was green mango: in a cooler, in a chutney with grilled fish, and finally in a
sorbet. Next month he's thinking about kochu.

## The details

- **When:** the last Thursday of every month, 7:30 PM
- **Where:** one long table in the private dining room, for sixteen guests
- **Price:** BDT 3,500 per guest (to confirm when you book)

Seats are booked one month at a time. Message us on WhatsApp or use the
[events enquiry form](/events/#enquire) to put your name down.
